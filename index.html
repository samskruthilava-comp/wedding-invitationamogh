The Venue

Casa del Lago

Via della Riva 14, Lake Como, Italy

View on Map

A lakeside villa of pale stone and long shuttered windows, held between the water and the hills. Its terraces step down through lemon trees to a quiet landing where the boats come in.

The gardens were laid out to be walked slowly. Gravel paths, clipped hedge, a fountain that has been running longer than anyone can account for.

In the late afternoon the light comes off the lake and turns the whole facade the colour of honey. That is the hour we chose.
